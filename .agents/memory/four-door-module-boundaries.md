## Current umbrella and delivery policy

When the owner refers to the already-separated WhatsApp-like calls/messages site, they mean the existing Helixly web messenger, not a request to create a new Android product.

**Why:** The owner corrected a mobile-versus-web interpretation and emphasized keeping citizens out of confusing Arena navigation.

**How to apply:** Continue the existing web messenger and preserve accounts and conversations; do not restart product/platform selection or repackage Android unless explicitly requested.

The official Helixly platform must continue to include every existing component and service. Separate tab links and identities are entry points within that platform, not permission to remove features or split away the main platform.

**Why:** The owner explicitly confirmed that division into tabs must preserve the complete official platform, with Clips (Hc) and Messages (Hm) validated first.

**How to apply:** Focus implementation and verification on those first two sections before starting work on the remaining tabs, then proceed one tab at a time. The newer homepage entries (Clips, Messages, Arena, Social) supersede the older four-door presentation described below; do not restore the older entry layout based on this historical note.

Extend the existing web/mobile implementation rather than rebuilding or replacing it when adding Android/iPhone background messaging and call support.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose continuing with what exists, without a rebuild, while discussing mobile background capabilities.

**How to apply:** Audit and reuse current integrations first; preserve accounts, conversations and platform components. This choice does not itself confirm background calling works or authorize paid account purchases.

## Earlier module ownership guidance

Arena Qaisypro keeps all existing features but presents four independent entry doors:
- AMIS is admin-only and owns site control, missions, 70/30 finance, wallet, and statistics.
- MESSAGERI owns public/group/legend and non-private-citizen messaging.
- SOCIAL owns citizen contacts and social communication tools.
- ARENA owns the remaining exploration areas, including the arena, missions, shop, profile, games, and GlobalStar.

The login surface doubles as the four-door choice page. Cross-module floating shells and embedded module content are not allowed.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected mixed navigation and wants free browsing through clear, independent product areas without deleting any existing tab.

**How to apply:** New UI and navigation must assign each feature to exactly one owner module. Preserve GlobalStar and all existing Arena routes unless the owner explicitly changes this decision.
