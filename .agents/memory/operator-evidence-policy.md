---
name: Operator evidence policy
description: Separate operator referrals, verified access, observed traffic and modeled savings.
---

Operator referrals indicate campaign attribution, never proof that a citizen has a particular pack or that GDNA is included in a restricted operator offer.

**Why:** The operator partnership request supplied prices, dial codes, savings percentages and market-share claims without authoritative evidence. Presenting those as confirmed would undermine the intended legal, evidence-based partnership pitch.

**How to apply:** Keep unverified pack details clearly marked and payments direct to official operators. Require actual agreements before claiming affiliate status or included access. Never introduce recruitment levels or downstream commissions.

Separate client-reported payload measurements, modeled bytes avoided, and network uptime. An application request-success ratio is not an independently measured carrier or global uptime SLA.

**Why:** A 90% saving or 99.99% availability claim is meaningful only with a defined baseline, observation window and sample. No Facebook-comparison benchmark was supplied, and bitrate estimates cannot establish perceptual quality equivalence.

**How to apply:** Show sample counts, periods, provenance and empty states. Use actual source/derivative metadata for estimates and require successful qualifying delivery before counting estimated avoided bytes. Predictions alone must not inflate historical savings.
