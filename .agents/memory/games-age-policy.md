---
name: Games age policy
description: Owner-mandated age-access boundary for registration, general platform access, and Games.
---

Registration and every non-game section must remain open to users of every age. Only Games and nested Games routes require age 18 or older. The required Games warning is: “هذا القسم يتطلب +18 حسب قوانين بلدك”.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected a global legal-age gate because it prevented younger citizens from registering and using unrelated parts of the empire.

**How to apply:** Persist date of birth, calculate eligibility on the server, and enforce it independently in every reward-bearing game API as well as the Games UI. Existing accounts without a date of birth may provide it once from Games; never gate non-game routes.
